How FarmInput Works

Your complete guide to using FarmInput

Getting Started is Easy

Follow these simple steps to begin your journey

1

Access the platform

Visit our website and click “Sign In” to access the mobile-optimized application

2

Create your Account

Register with your phone number, verify with OTP, and complete your farmer profile

3

Complete Onboarding

Set up your farm details, preferences, and start exploring the platform features

4

Start Managing

Begin logging inputs, tracking spending, and joining buying groups to save money

Placing Orders Made Simple

Multiple ways to purchase your farm inputs

Individual Purchases

1

Browse Suppliers

Navigate to the Supplier Directory and browse verified suppliers in your region

2

Compare Prices

Use the Price Comparison tool to find the contact information provided

3

Contact Supplier

Call or message the supplier directly using the contact information provided

4

Log Your Purchase

After buying, log the input in the app to track to track your spending automatically

Group Purchases

Save up to 20%

1

Find or Create a Group

Navigate to the Supplier Directory and browse verified suppliers in your region

2

Join the Group

Request to join and chat with other members to coordinate the purchase

3

Confirm Your Quantity

Specify how much you need and see your discount price automatically

4

Complete Group Order

Once minimum quantity is met, the group order is placed with the supplier

How Deliveries work

Efficient delivery to get inputs when you need them

Order Confirmation

After order placement, you receive confirmation with estimated delivery date and pickup location

Order Processing

Supplier prepares your order. For group purchases, all member orders are consolidated

Pickup/Delivery

Collect your inputs from designated pickup point or arrange direct delivery to your farm

Stay Updated

Track your order status in real-time through the app. Get notifications when your order is ready for pickup and coordinate with other group members for collection.




Key Features

Everything you need to manage farm inputs efficiently

Input Logging

Record all purchases with details like quantity, price, supplier, and date for comprehensive tracking

Supplier Directory

Access verified suppliers with ratings, reviews, locations, and contact information

Spending Analytics

View detailed charts and trends of your agricultural spending over time with insights

Buying Groups

Join or create groups with other farmers to access bulk discounts and save money

Price Comparison

Compare prices across regions and suppliers to find the best deals on farm inputs

Mobile Access

Full mobile optimization lets you manage everything on-the-go from your smartphone
